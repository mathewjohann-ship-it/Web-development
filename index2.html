Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • Basmati rice
  • Natural yoghurt
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Green chillies
  • Ginger and garlic paste
  • Turmeric powder
  • Chilli powder
  • Ground coriander
  • Ground cumin
  • Garam masala
  • Bay leaves
  • Cardamom pods
  • Cloves
  • Cinnamon stick
  • Fresh coriander
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • Lemon juice
  • Cooking oil
  • Ghee or butter
  • Salt
  • Saffron (optional)
  • Milk (optional)

Recipe Instructions

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yoghurt, ginger and garlic paste, turmeric, chilli powder, coriander, cumin, garam masala, salt and lemon juice.
  3. Leave the chicken to marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Heat oil in a large cooking pot and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  5. Remove half of the fried onions and keep them aside.
  6. Add ginger and garlic paste, chopped tomatoes and green chillies to the pot.
  7. Add the marinated chicken and cook for approximately 10–15 minutes.
  8. In a separate pot, boil water with bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and salt.
  9. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is approximately 70% cooked. Drain the rice.
  10. Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken mixture.
  11. Add fried onions, fresh coriander, mint leaves and ghee.
  12. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes.
  13. Turn off the heat and leave the biryani covered for another 10 minutes.
  14. Gently mix and serve hot.

Ingredient Preparation

Basmati rice:
Wash the rice until the water runs clear and soak it in water for 20–30 minutes.
Onions:
Peel and thinly slice the onions before frying them until golden brown.
Tomatoes:
Wash and chop the tomatoes into small pieces.
Green chillies:
Wash and slit the chillies lengthways.
Ginger and garlic:
Peel and blend the ginger and garlic into a smooth paste, if not using ready-made paste.
Fresh coriander:
Wash and finely chop the coriander leaves.
Fresh mint:
Wash the mint leaves and remove any thick stems.
Whole spices:
Prepare the bay leaves, cardamom pods, cloves and cinnamon stick for flavouring the rice.
Saffron:
Soak a pinch of saffron in warm milk for extra colour and fragrance (optional).
